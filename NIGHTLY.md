### What's new in this batch

Flash the **merged bin at 0x0** (erase first): each nightly is a fresh wipe-and-flash.

- **Selection highlight fixed:** [ CORE ] on HOME no longer looks selected all the time.
  Only the item the trackball points at is highlighted, and the highlight moves as you roll.
  After a touch nothing stays lit; a tapped button only flashes while you press it. The first
  roll or click after a touch just shows where the selection is. This also applies to the
  CORE / TASTIC bottom buttons (DMs / ADVERT / EDIT), the lists and the popups.
- **Themes:** SETUP > DISPLAY > THEME cycles four presets: **CLASSIC CYBER** (green on black,
  the default), **NIGHT MODE** (dim warm red on black), **WIN98** (navy on silver gray) and
  **HIGH CONTRAST** (black on white). Each one has its own selection, pressed and warning
  colours, and the change applies straight away. If a theme ever makes the screen hard to
  read, press **BACKSPACE** (or hold the trackball) on the boot screen to go back to CLASSIC
  CYBER.
- **Lock screen ignores touch:** while the deck is locked, the touchscreen does nothing. It
  doesn't wake the screen or keep it on. Press any key or use the trackball to wake it, then
  hold the trackball 3 s to unlock as before. Touch works again after unlocking.
